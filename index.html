FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the cost of your cleaning services in St. Petersburg?

The cost of our cleaning services in St. Petersburg, Florida depends on the type of service you choose, any additional services, and the overall size of your home or commercial building. However, you can be confident that our pricing is more than fair, while the quality of our service remains top-notch. Before we begin, we always provide a free estimate and make sure everything meets your expectations and fits within your planned budget. You'll know exactly what to expect.

Are all cleaning supplies included in the service?

Yes, all cleaning supplies are included in the service at no additional cost. You don't need to worry about a thing — we bring everything necessary, including cleaning products and professional equipment suited to your type of cleaning. During our initial free estimate, we'll assess your space to make sure we come fully prepared, based on your home's needs and your preferences.

Do I need to be present during the cleaning?

If it's more convenient for you, you don't need to be present during the cleaning — such as when you've scheduled a service while you're at work. Even for the first cleaning, your presence isn't required because we'll first visit you for a free estimate. During that visit, we'll have a chance to meet in person, and you can share any specific preferences or highlight areas that need special attention.

Do you offer cleaning services on weekends in St. Petersburg?

Absolutely! We're here for you 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Reach out to us anytime — we're always available and happy to help.
